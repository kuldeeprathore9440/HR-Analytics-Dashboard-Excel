HR Analytics Dashboard using Excel

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a professional Business Intelligence project developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze employee data and generate actionable workforce insights. The dashboard helps HR teams and business stakeholders monitor employee attrition, workforce demographics, departmental performance, and employee distribution through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

The primary objective of this project is to support data-driven HR decision-making by identifying workforce trends, high-risk departments, and employee attrition patterns. The dashboard transforms raw HR data into meaningful insights using Excel analytics and visualization techniques.
